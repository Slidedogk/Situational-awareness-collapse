## 6. Status (as of 2026-08-23) — HONEST POST-MORTEM framing
- The **original book is gone**: public equities sold to Citadel (Jul 30); the buyer itself unwound >80% of the risk within three weeks (Aug 21).
- The **equity was largely destroyed**: ~67% of the fund lost in July 2026 (press; exact % UNCONFIRMED).
- The **legal entity is not dead**: Situational Awareness LP filed a 13F (period 2026-06-30) and a 13G/A (2026-08-14) — the shell, the GP, and the manager are still acting. It retains (per press) its **Anthropic private stakes** and is still deploying capital (Source Foundry $400M).
- Conclusion the site states: **"The $45B fund ceased. The firm did not."**
